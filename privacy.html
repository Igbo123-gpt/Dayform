Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Dayform handles when you use the website. Please read it and contact us if you have questions.

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Information you provide

If you choose to sign in with Google, Dayform receives basic profile information made available by Google, such as your name, email address and profile picture. We use this information to identify your signed-in session.

Habit information and device storage

Habit names, schedules, completion records and reminder preferences may be stored in your browser's local storage by the current version of Dayform. This means they are associated with the browser and device you use. Clearing browser data or changing devices may remove or make that information unavailable. Do not assume your habits are backed up or synchronised across devices.

Cookies and sessions

When you sign in, Dayform uses a session cookie to maintain your signed-in state. The cookie is intended to be used for authentication and is not an advertising cookie.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, your browser may ask for permission to show notifications. Reminder settings are stored in your browser. You can change notification permissions in your browser settings.

Advertising

Dayform may display third-party advertising in the future, including advertising provided by Google AdSense. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to your location, consent choices and Google's policies. This statement describes a planned feature; it does not mean AdSense is currently active.

Third-party services

Google Sign-In is used when you choose to authenticate with Google. Google's handling of information is also governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by Dayform. The current app uses a basic server-side session configuration and is still being prepared for public launch. We will update this policy if the app's data storage or processing changes.

Children's privacy

Dayform is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the situation.

Your choices

You can choose not to sign in, sign out, manage browser notification permissions, and clear locally stored browser data. Contact us with privacy questions or requests.
